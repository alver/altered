How to play

  • Each Day: Morning (draw + Mana) → Afternoon (play cards) → Dusk → Night.
  • In the Afternoon, play one card per turn or Pass.
  • Characters join your Hero or Companion Expedition.
  • At Dusk, beat the facing Expedition in one of your region's terrains to advance.
  • Win when your two markers meet on the track.
